Guard

Type: Enemy

Threat Level: Professional

Description

Not manufactured, not raised from the dead, not somebody's confused ex-soldier — Guardians are the real deal, and they know it. Every one goes through the same brutal training before deployment; early batches, by all accounts, just... stood there. This one clearly graduated. Studies you, retreats when it's smart, comes back swinging when it isn't. The only fight in this arena that isn't rigged in your favor.
