Slime

Type: Enemy

Threat Level: Committed

Description

Distant cousin of the Slimes, apparently — same place of origin, none of the patience. Chargers don't chase you so much as lock onto where you were the instant they noticed you, then commit to that decision with their entire body, wall or no wall. Watching one faceplant at full speed remains the closest thing this arena offers to a comedy act.
