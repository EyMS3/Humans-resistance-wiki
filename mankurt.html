Mankurt

Type: Enemy

Threat Level: Confused but Furious

Description

Once a person. Still technically a person, if you squint — just one with several key parts missing, chief among them a name. Mankurts wander with no memory of what they were doing before you showed up, and fill that blank space with one very focused idea: hit you. The uniform's a nice touch, though. Somebody's, once.
