Bomber

Type: Enemy

Threat Level: Extremely Temporary

Description

Technically sapient. Practically not the brightest guest at this particular party. Somebody else signed these guys up for arena duty, and it definitely wasn't them. Bombers shuffle toward you with the calm, unbothered energy of someone who has never once weighed the consequences of their actions — because doing that was never really their job. Once they touch you, the countdown starts. Recommended response: leave. Quickly.
